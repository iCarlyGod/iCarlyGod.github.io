Hi. I've been forgetting to post here.  I get I promised to post stuff like six months ago (exactly too) and I haven't, but that's because I thought the stuff I had drafted for about a month or so was unnecessarily edgy and cynical, so I scrapped most of them.  However, there has been some other recent developments in my life, so I thought I should update this in terms of what's been happening and how it will affect things here going forward.  

The first is that I've started to coach Splatoon teams as of like four months ago.  It's been enjoyable and I've discovered aspects of the game for me to explore in directions I was previously trying to progress blindly through.  Because coaching forced me to simplify and articulate the abstract tactical and psychological theory I've subconciously developed over the years, it has allowed me to think through and reflect on how I see the game of Splatoon.  I still think the game is flawed competitively, but it's the game I have the most skill in and so it's one of few games I can really test and explore the new ideas I find.

Second is that I was busy with school up until mid-August, so I wasn't able to get much time to make blog posts.  Since then, I was supposed to get a co-op job, but I didn't, so I had to spend a lot of time thinking about what to do until January.  I've tried a little bit of game development, I've tried making and editing YouTube videos, and I've tried studying game design.  Nothing has stuck very much, but I've ended up making a bunch of rambles I guess I can convert into blog posts, so there's that I guess.

Lastly, I've been getting into Guilty Gear Strive.  One of the Splatoon teams I coached played it as like a team-bonding game, so I bought it on sale and tried it out.  In case you are wondering, I play Ramlethal.  It's funny because I only played her originally because I had no DLC and selected her on random, but I really like her as a character in terms of both in gameplay and appearence.  Anyway, the reason this is significant is because it has opened my mind up to learning fighting game concepts.  It also helps with my coaching.  I only coach people who are worse than me, because I need practice and experience to develop myself, so practicing a game in a genre I have little prior experience in allows me to experience the struggles of learning a game.  It's been fun and it has opened up for a new dimension of ideas to think about in respect to other games I play.

Anyway, that's what's been happening with me as of recent. I'll try and at least throw more stuff in here so this site isn't as empty.  And yeah, Have a good one.
